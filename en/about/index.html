Foundation models · Commerce intelligence

Senior Staff Machine Learning Engineer at Shopify

Yang Liu 刘阳

Yang Liu is a Senior Staff Machine Learning Engineer on Shopify’s Foundation Model team, where he works on generative search and recommendation, large-scale representation learning, and embeddings.

Before Shopify, he built machine learning systems at Magic Leap and at Sportlogiq prior to its acquisition. His work turns foundation-model research into reliable products operating at commerce scale.
